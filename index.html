Privacy Policy — TrialGuard

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Contact: support@trialguard.app

Who we are

TrialGuard is a local-first free trial and subscription renewal reminder tool. It helps you remember to cancel trials before you are charged. TrialGuard is sold as a paid store app (one-time purchase at download).

What TrialGuard does NOT do

  • We do not connect to your bank or payment accounts.
  • We do not cancel subscriptions for you.
  • We do not provide financial, legal, or tax advice.
  • We do not guarantee that you will save money.

Data we store

By default, TrialGuard stores the following on your device only:

  • Trial / subscription names you enter
  • Amounts, currencies, billing cycles, and end dates you enter
  • Optional cancel URLs and cancel notes you enter
  • App preferences (language, reminder offsets)

Optional AI Smart Parse (BYOK): if enabled, your API key and endpoint stay on device; requests go to the URL you configure. We do not operate an AI backend for this feature.

We do not require an account. We do not upload your trial list to our servers in the MVP.

Optional analytics / crash reporting

If a crash-reporting DSN is configured in a release build (for example Sentry), anonymous crash reports may collect app version, device OS version, aggregate feature usage events, and crash stack traces. Core features remain usable without analytics.

Purchases

The app purchase is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play when you download TrialGuard. There is no in-app purchase or restore flow inside the app.

Your choices

  • Delete individual trials inside the app
  • Use Clear all local data in Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data
  • Manage notification permissions in system settings
  • Disable or clear optional BYOK settings

Legal bases (GDPR) / CCPA / Children

Where GDPR applies, we process local app data to provide the service. We do not sell personal information. TrialGuard is not directed at children under 13.
